3. How each point you raised has been resolved
   - Producer / country: every feature now carries ProducerName "PHUOC HOA KAMPONG THOM PROCESSING FACTORY"
     and ProducerCountry "KH". Plot codes 5.14PK / 5.14PH are internal codes of our plantation.
   - Schema: each feature carries only ProducerName, ProducerCountry, ProductionPlace, Area and PlotName.
     The internal plantation-inventory fields have been removed.
   - Plot identifiers: ProductionPlace is the unique plot code. Plots made of several separate parcels are
     split into one Polygon per parcel with suffixes -1, -2, -3. There are no duplicated codes
     (434 production places for 406 plots).
   - Area: Area is the geodesic area of each polygon, so declared and measured areas match exactly.
   - Overlaps: none. Two small sub-plots (H6S inside H6T, J6S inside J6T) are now excluded from the
     surrounding plot, so H6T contains an inner ring.
   - Geometry: Polygon only, WGS84, 2D (no altitude), at most 6 decimals, no consecutive duplicate points,
     all rings closed, all coordinates in Cambodia.
   - Completeness: the 28/10/2025 plantation DDS listed 406 plots, but the old map contained polygons for
     only 262 of them. The revised map covers all 406 declared plots (plot "G8" corresponds to parcel
     G8N).
